📷 Pocket-Cam v0.4

One iPhone camera, one browser viewer. Private peer-to-peer WebRTC streaming.

Quick Pair · Experimental

No offer/answer copying. Start on iPhone, then open its private viewer link on iPad or PC.

Applies when broadcasting starts; camera and resolution can also change live.

Quick Pair uses the free public PeerJS signalling service and Google STUN; video is direct peer-to-peer where possible. No TURN relay is configured, so some networks won't connect. The private link is your access key; don't share it publicly.

Manual pairing fallback (v0.1)
Ready. Keep the iPhone unlocked and Safari in the foreground.

For v0.1, exchange the connection descriptions privately (for example with a message or AirDrop). No video goes through GitHub. Uses a public STUN server for network discovery; some mobile or restrictive networks will require a TURN relay, which is not included in this build.
